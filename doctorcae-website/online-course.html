Online Course for Parents

The Body First Framework: 7 Steps to Helping Kids and Teens Regulate Emotions and Behavior

Move beyond managing behavior and learn how to respond to your child's nervous system with clarity, confidence, and calm.

If you've found yourself reacting in the moment and then second-guessing later…
or trying strategy after strategy without lasting change…
you're not alone.

Most parenting approaches focus on behavior.

But behavior is often the last thing to shift.

This course offers a different starting point.

Inside, you'll learn how to understand your child's behavior through the lens of the nervous system — and how to respond in ways that actually support regulation, connection, and long-term change.

This isn't about doing more.
It's about seeing differently, slowing down, and building the capacity — in both you and your child — for something to shift.

What You'll Learn

  • How to recognize when your child is dysregulated (and what that actually means)
  • How to respond in the moment without escalating the situation
  • How your own nervous system shapes your child's behavior
  • Why traditional strategies often don't stick — and what to do instead
  • How to create small, repeatable moments of safety that lead to change over time
  • How to move from reacting → to responding with intention

What This Course Is (and Isn't)

This course is:

  • grounded in nervous system science
  • practical and applicable to everyday moments
  • designed for real-life parenting (not perfection)

This course is not:

  • a quick fix
  • a list of behavior strategies
  • about getting your child to "comply"

Who This Is For

This course is for parents who:

  • feel overwhelmed by their child's big emotions or behaviors
  • notice patterns that keep repeating
  • want to respond differently, but don't know how in the moment
  • are ready to move beyond surface-level strategies
  • want a deeper, more connected way of supporting their child

You don't need to have the right words.
You don't need to get it perfect.
You just need a different starting point.
